# Marketing product-truth matrix

This package is presentation-only. Product authorization, limits, provider access, billing, and activity generation remain owned by the authenticated app. Marketing copy is intentionally bounded to the frozen transition contract.

| Public claim | Truth boundary | Marketing treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personalized training from real games | Existing provider import/findings contracts | Described as a capability; no fabricated counts or outcomes |
| Free tier | One starter pack; metered Tempo/Battery/Daily experience; one provider sample up to 10 recent games | Exact limits shown on pricing |
| Pro tier | One `blundr_pro` entitlement; annual `blundr_pro_annual`; monthly `blundr_pro_monthly` | Exact price, trial, renewal and app-domain purchase handoff |
| Daily Blundr | Tempo, Battery and Daily activities are existing product concepts | Explained separately from standalone minigames |
| Standalone minigames | Production catalog is exactly Deep Tactic Shots, Knight Gymnasium, and King & Pawn Lab | Exactly three listed; legacy eight-game registry not exposed |
| Provider relationship | Chess.com and Lichess are optional connections and trademark owners | Password-free language plus independent-service disclaimer |
| Support and deletion | Existing app/account contracts; no approved marketing form transport | Truthful mailto fallback; no fake submission success |

No Supabase, provider, RevenueCat, service-role, hidden-solution, or authenticated API code belongs in this package.
