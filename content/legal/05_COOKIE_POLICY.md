# Blundr Cookie and Similar Technologies Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Policy explains how Blundr uses cookies, local storage, pixels, and similar technologies on blundr.io and related web services. It should be read with the Blundr Privacy Policy.

## 1. Necessary technologies

Blundr uses technologies that are reasonably necessary to provide the Service, including authentication, session continuity, account security, fraud prevention, request integrity, load balancing, billing handoff, consent-state storage, and essential user settings. Where applicable law allows, these technologies operate without optional consent because the requested Service cannot function properly without them.

## 2. Preference technologies

We may use first-party storage to remember choices that you ask us to remember, such as interface preferences, training preferences, or cookie choices. Where law requires consent for a particular preference technology, we will request it before using that technology.

## 3. Analytics technologies

Blundr may use analytics to understand aggregate product usage, reliability, performance, and conversion. Where consent is legally required, analytics technologies will be disabled until you affirmatively choose to allow them.

Analytics consent is optional. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the core Service. You may withdraw consent at any time through Cookie or Privacy Settings.

## 4. Advertising technologies

At launch, Blundr does not use third-party behavioral-advertising cookies and does not use personal information for targeted advertising based on activity across unrelated businesses or services.

If that practice changes, we will update this Policy and provide the consent or opt-out controls required by law before the change takes effect.

## 5. Your choices

Where a cookie banner is required, it will provide clear choices to accept optional analytics, reject optional analytics, or manage preferences. Non-essential technologies will not be enabled before consent in jurisdictions that require prior consent.

You can change your selection later through the Cookie or Privacy Settings link in the footer or account settings. You may also use browser controls to block or delete cookies, although blocking necessary technologies may prevent parts of the Service from functioning.

## 6. Browser privacy signals

Blundr honors legally required browser-based privacy signals for activities to which those signals apply. At launch, Blundr does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no sale/sharing activity to opt out of.

## 7. Cookie inventory

The live Cookie Settings interface must identify the cookies or similar technologies actually in use, including their provider, purpose, category, and typical duration. The inventory must be generated from the deployed Service and kept current when vendors or technologies change.
