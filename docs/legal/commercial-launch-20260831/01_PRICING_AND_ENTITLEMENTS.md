# Blundr Launch Pricing, Entitlements, and Onboarding Paywall

Last updated: August 31, 2026

## Pricing

### Blundr Free
$0. No payment method required.

### Blundr Pro Monthly
$9.99 per month, plus applicable taxes.

### Blundr Pro Annual
$69.99 per year, plus applicable taxes. Equivalent to approximately $5.83 per month and approximately 42% less than paying the monthly price for 12 months.

### Pro Trial
New eligible customers may start Blundr Pro with a 7-day free trial. A valid payment method is required. The trial begins when checkout is successfully completed. Unless canceled before the trial ends, the subscription automatically converts to the selected paid plan and renews automatically until canceled.

### Downgrade Rules

Downgrading never deletes chess progress, repertoire history, review items, rewards, or opening unlocks. If a Pro member has more than three active openings, the account must choose up to three active openings for Free; all other data remains preserved. If a Pro member has a Daily target above five, the stored preference may be retained but the effective Free limit becomes five until Pro is restored. Review items above the Free daily allowance remain queued rather than being discarded.

No account may be created for a person under 16 through the standard launch flow.

## Billing UX Rules

- Never precheck a subscription-consent box.
- Never hide the Free option.
- Do not preselect Monthly or Annual; require an affirmative plan choice.
- Show $0 due today, trial end date, first charge amount, billing frequency, taxes, renewal, and cancellation terms before collecting final subscription consent.
- Send an immediate durable confirmation email after trial enrollment.
- Send a trial-ending reminder approximately 72 hours before the first charge.
- Send annual renewal reminders 30 days before annual renewal.
- Send a six-month subscription reminder to monthly subscribers before the next monthly renewal.
- Provide online cancellation in Settings → Billing without requiring chat, email, or telephone contact.
- Send a cancellation confirmation immediately after cancellation.
- A trial canceled before conversion remains Pro through the stated trial end and incurs no subscription charge.
- A paid subscription canceled normally remains Pro through the paid billing period unless applicable law requires another result.
- Offer a 14-day refund window after the first paid charge following a trial and after each annual renewal. Mandatory local refund, cooling-off, and withdrawal rights apply if they are more favorable.
- If renewal payment fails, use a reasonable retry/grace period and disclose any downgrade. Do not delete user data because of payment failure.
